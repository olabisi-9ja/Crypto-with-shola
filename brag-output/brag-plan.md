# Brag plan — Crypto With Shola

**What:** A crypto education community (free lessons, Telegram group, member area with signals) run by Shola, Lagos.
**For:** Curious beginners who want to understand the market, not chase pumps.
**Different:** Anti-hype. Publishes losses too ("-2.1% (SL hit) — Lesson: …").
**Angle:** In a genre full of Lambo promises, the brag is *no shortcut*. A crypto brand that's proud of its process.
**Hook:** "There is no shortcut." slams onto ink, then "There is a process." in acid italic.
**Tone:** default: punchy, playful, clean. Hard-ish cuts on the beat.
**Identity:** ink #151515, paper #f7f3e9, acid #e8ff40, orange #ff6339; Syne display, Georgia italic accents, DM Mono labels, DM Sans body. Real portrait, ₿ sun, ✦ star, ticker.
**Share caption:** "Crypto education with no shortcut. Just a process."
